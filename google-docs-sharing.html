How this works

Do this in a group of three. Everyone shares with everyone, so you will send files and receive them from two people. Work at the same pace as your group: many tasks need your teammates to finish a step first. Keep this page on the right side of your screen and Google Drive on the left. Tick each box when you finish.

Everything here works the same on a Windows computer and on a Chromebook, because sharing all happens in the web browser.
