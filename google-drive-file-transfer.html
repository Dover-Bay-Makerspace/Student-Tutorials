How this works

You will move files both ways between Google Drive (online) and your H: drive (your school storage). Keep this page on the right side of the screen and do each task on your real computer. Tick each box when you finish. Some boxes tick themselves when this page sees the right files.

H: drive— upload →Google Drive
Google Drive— download →Downloads (C:)— move →H: drive

Anything you download lands in Downloads on the C: drive first, and the C: drive gets erased. A download isn't safe until you move it to H:.
